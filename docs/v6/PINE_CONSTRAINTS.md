## Consequences baked into the V6 plan

1. **Entry model** — C-07 + C-08 force MARKET_ON_CONFIRM with `process_orders_on_close=true`
   (see `GOLDEN_SIGNALS.md` §1 for the parity impact).
2. **Request budget** — ≈ 7 unique requests, 1 foreign symbol (MES, data only). C-01/C-02 are not
   a risk; C-05 (1m intrabars for the ICT_OB) is the one feature that may be plan-limited — the
   fallback is the 5m sweep-bar OB (PORT_MAP O9, drift ≤ App. E ± 4 ticks).
3. **No repaint** — C-03/C-17: every HTF value is the previous completed bar (`[1]`, `lookahead_off`);
   entries only on `barstate.isconfirmed`; `calc_on_every_tick=false`.
4. **Operational** — C-09 (re-create the alert after any edit), C-13 (2-month expiry below Premium),
   C-12 (paid plan + 2FA; exact tier → `docs/PLATFORM_FACTS.md`).
5. **Open items for Agent 03** — C-14 session reset moment of `strategy.risk.*` (verify in OA-2).

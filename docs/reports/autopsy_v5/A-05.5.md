## 1. THE FIX

**New config keys.** Both are registered in `EngineConfig.KEYS`, set in `engine-defaults.properties`, printed in the boot-table OTE line and in `OteConfig.describe()`, and read via `OteConfig`.

| key | default | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `ote.pdArraySource` | `ICT_OB` | `ICT_OB` adds the ICT order block below. `SWEEP_BAR` is the exact 05.2 behaviour, kept for A/B. |
| `ote.obLookbackBars` | `5` | Bars walked back from the sweep bar (clamped 1..30). |

**The ICT order block** (`OteSetupDriver.ictOrderBlock`):
- **Timeframe.** It is read on the **1m feed timeframe**. That is where `ctx.sweep` is stamped and where 05.2 reads the rejection wick (`sweepBar`).
- **Snapshot.** When the sweep bar is recorded, the driver snapshots the `ote.obLookbackBars` 1m bars printed **before** it.
- **Selection.** The OB is the **newest opposite-close candle** in that window: down-close for a long, up-close for a short. That is the last opposite candle before the move that swept the level reversed.
- **Only that newest candle counts.** Older opposite candles are not OBs. Like every other candidate, it must **overlap the OTE band** (`PdArrayLocator.overlaps`).
- **Other candidates.** The 05.2 candidates stay exactly as they are: the 5m sweep-bar OB, the rejection WICK (≥ 0.5 of range) and band-overlapping FVG / IFVG / BREAKER that reach the swept level.
- **Reaction.** Unchanged: `impulseReaction`, a close back beyond the swept level in the trade direction.

**Entry rule (stated).** An OB enters at its **mean threshold (50 %)**, clamped into the band. This is Agent 04's existing `PdArrayLocator.entryLevel`.
- For the 10:06 long that is (30755.00 + 30803.50) / 2 = **30779.25**.
- The brief called 30779.25 the "OB high". The 10:05 high is actually 30803.50, which sits above the 0.618 at 30801.50. 30779.25 is the OB's mean.
- An edge-at-the-band rule would have given 30801.50: 2 micros, filled 10:07, the same 10:14 stop.

**Stop.** `ote.stopMode=BAND` is untouched: min(0.786, PD-array far edge, retrace extreme) − 4 ticks = min(30747.5, 30755.0, 30752.0) − 1.00 = **30746.50**. Stops are not widened.

**Candidate priority (deviation from the brief, measured).** I implemented and ran the brief's strict "OB nearest the swept level → WICK → FVG" order first. On the tape it broke the "no other session's fills may drop" rule:

| case | effect of the strict order |
|---|---|
| 09-28 12:32 NY_LUNCH short (Main: +$156) | **Lost.** The 5m sweep OB [30565, 30642.5] contains the swept 30640, so the strict order prefers it over the IFVG [30645, 30678.5]. Its mean 30603.75 clamps to the 0.618 at 30605.5, which gives RR(T1) (30605.5 − 30558) / (30723 − 30605.5) = 0.40 < 1.0, and M7 refuses every bar. |
| 09-23 01:42 ASIA short (Main: +$51) | Moved off its 31036.0 wick entry to the ICT OB mean 31033.5. It became a −$95 loss. |

**So the pick stays Agent 04's `bestInBand`: entry nearest the 0.705, ties go to the OB.** The ICT OB is added as a candidate. Where it is the only candidate (09-25 10:06, 09-23 06:46) or the nearest to the 0.705 (G1 14:53), it wins. Everywhere else Main's pick is unchanged.

**Refusals keep writing lastGateFailed (05.4 rule).** `impulse-no-pd-array-at-sweep` still writes `ALARM: …` plus a GateDecisionEvent; the text now says "no sweep/ICT OB". A later M7 refusal writes `M7…`. `ArmedNeverSilentTapeTest.noOteArmedBarIsSilent` still passes under the new default: 0 silent armed bars.

**Transcript and events.** Under ICT_OB, the `OteAlarmEvent` reaction carries the pick (`| pd OB [30755.0,30803.5]@2026-09-25T14:05:00Z src=ICT_OB`). `OteSetupDriver.lastPdPick()` exposes the same string.

## 1. ROOT CAUSE (G2, 2026-09-25)

`DealingRangeTracker` built the range over the whole Globex trading day, from Thu 18:00. On 09-25 that day's extremes are both **overnight**:
- low **30679.00**, Thu 20:15;
- high **30999.50**, Fri 03:35.

So from 03:35 the range is BULLISH [30679.00, 30999.50]. The RTH session never closes below 30679 (its low is 30684.00), so the range stayed BULLISH all day. The engine bought the 10:06 LOW sweep of 30752 in the long band [30747.5, 30801.5] of that leg, and the trade was stopped out at −1R.

The owner draws the NY-AM impulse instead. Tape 5m bars:
- HH **30926.50** (09:55 bar; the owner's 09:45 marker is on the PT chart);
- LL **30684.00** (10:15–10:19 bars; marker 30721 at 10:30).

That impulse is BEARISH. On G1 (09-28) both extremes form after 09:30, so both windows give 30759.25 / 30356.75.

## 2. THE FIX

**New keys.** All are registered in `EngineConfig.KEYS` (boot table, `/api/status effectiveConfig`), set in `engine-defaults.properties` and read through `BiasConfig`. `grep -rn "System.getProperty" trading-engine/src/main` returns 0 hits outside `EngineConfigLoader`.

| key | default | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `bias.range.window` | `AUTO` | `SESSION_DAY`: the Globex day from 18:00 (the Agent 03 behaviour, A/B). `RTH_FIRST`: from 09:30 the RTH leg is published; below the minimum it is non-decisive, with direction carried from the session day. `AUTO`: the RTH leg once it spans the minimum, else the session day. |
| `bias.range.minLegTicks` | `400` | Minimum RTH impulse, in ticks (MNQ: 100 pt). |
| `bias.range.minLegTicks.<SYM>` | (base) | Per-symbol override. |

**`DealingRangeTracker`:**
- The session-day logic is **untouched**. SESSION_DAY is byte-identical to Main; see §6.
- A second `Leg` is seeded at the first bar at or after **09:30** of each trading day. It is fed until the 17:00 halt and dropped at the 18:00 roll, so Asia, London and pre-NY never see it.
- The `Leg` runs the **same rules** as the session day:
  - a new extreme extends the leg;
  - a 1m close beyond the far extreme flips it;
  - a pullback of `reanchorFraction` re-anchors it.
- It becomes decisive once `hi − lo ≥ minLegTicks × tick`, with the high and low on different bars: HH→LL is BEARISH and LL→HH is BULLISH.
- `snapshot()` returns the governing leg:
  - **SESSION_DAY**: always the session day.
  - **RTH_FIRST**: the RTH leg from 09:30.
  - **AUTO**: the RTH leg once it is decisive, else the session day.
- `StdvOteRunnerStrategy` builds the tracker with `DealingRangeTracker.fromConfig(symbol, spec.tickSize())`. `OteGoldenReplay` builds it the same way.

**Why a 400-tick minimum, not 100:** the threshold sweep below. At 100, 200 and 300 ticks the 09-22 09:30–09:31 opening bars (LL 30763.50 → HH 30847.00, 83.5 pt in two bars) become "the impulse". The anchor of an in-flight bearish setup then switches under it, and it shorts 09:31 at 30820 into a 13-pt stop (−1R, −$130). At 400 the leg is decided one bar later. By then M2b refuses that short: GATE-M2b 09:35–09:44, then an HTF bias flip at 09:45. 400 and 600 give identical trades, so the result sits on a plateau.

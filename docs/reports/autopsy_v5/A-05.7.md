## 1. DEFECT (LIVE, `logs/engine-live-20260928-164649.log`)

**What happened:**
1. At the 18:00 ET Globex reopen, `DealingRangeTracker` started a new session-day range from scratch.
2. `bias.range.minRangePct` = 0.08 % is about 24.5 pt at 30,600. The first 78.25-pt up-move of Asia, **30537.00 → 30615.25**, made that fresh range decisive **BULLISH**.
3. The RTH session had just printed the bearish impulse **30759.25 → 30356.75** and closed weak.
4. For the first hour of Asia the `[GATES MNQ]` lines read `range:BULLISH[30537.0-30615.25]`.
5. The engine armed a long, and M2b refused it on every bar.
6. The range flipped `BEARISH[30521.5-30615.25]` only once price broke the micro-range low.

**Where it came from:** the AUTO window (Agent 05.6) drops the RTH leg at the 18:00 roll. The session-day range then restarts. That is by design for the day's own range, but nothing carried the governing range across the roll.

## 2. THE FIX

**Rule** (`DealingRangeTracker`, only when `bias.range.carryAcrossReopen=true`, the default):

1. **Capture.** At the session-day rollover (the first bar at or after 18:00 ET), the tracker captures the previous session's **governing** range as of its close: `snapshot()` taken before the roll. That is:
   - the RTH impulse leg under AUTO once it was decisive (RTH_FIRST: the RTH leg);
   - else the session-day range;
   - else the range that session itself was still carrying (a quiet session carries on).
2. **Govern.** That high, low and direction keep governing, published `decisive=true`.
3. **Extend.** A print beyond the carried range extends it: a new high raises the high and a new low lowers the low. **The direction does not flip on it.**
4. **Hand over.** A new-session leg is seeded at the reopen bar. It runs the same rules as the 05.6 RTH leg: a new extreme extends it, a close beyond the far extreme flips it, a pullback re-anchors it. It becomes decisive when `hi − lo ≥ bias.range.minLegTicks × tick` (MNQ 400 ticks = 100 pt), with the high and low on different bars. On that bar the carry ends, and the published range is **exactly the flag-off range from then on**. An opposite impulse of at least 100 pt is therefore the only way the carried direction is replaced.
5. **RTH leg.** An AUTO RTH leg that becomes decisive also governs over the carry, because it is itself such an impulse. In practice the session leg, which started at 18:00, is always decisive first.
6. **Cold start.** A cold start (no previous session) and `reset()` carry nothing.

**Key:**

| key | default | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `bias.range.carryAcrossReopen` | `true` | Keep the previous session's governing range across the 18:00 ET reopen until the new session prints an impulse ≥ `bias.range.minLegTicks`. `false` = restart at the reopen (Main behaviour, A/B). |

It is registered in `EngineConfig.KEYS` (Type BOOL, so it appears in the boot table and in `/api/status effectiveConfig`). It is set in `engine-defaults.properties` and read through `BiasConfig.rangeCarryAcrossReopen()`. `DealingRangeTracker.fromConfig(symbol, tick)` passes it in.
- The 2- and 4-argument constructors keep the flag **off**, so every pre-05.7 unit fixture is unchanged.
- `grep -rn "System.getProperty" trading-engine/src/main` returns **0 hits outside `EngineConfigLoader`**.

## 4. MEASUREMENT FIX: EVERY BUS SIGNAL IS HANDLED ON ITS BAR (test code only)

**The defect:** `FunnelAutopsyHarness` drained the signal queue only on bars where the state went `≠IN_TRADE → IN_TRADE`.
- A re-arm after a close can publish a signal on a bar that reads `IN_TRADE → IN_TRADE`. On the carry run that happened at 09-23 05:16: the 05:04 long closed at 05:10 and a new long was emitted at 05:16.
- Such a signal sat in the queue. It was then submitted at the NEXT transition with **stale prices**. The first carry run showed a "09-25 11:33 LONG e=31014.0" with price at 30848, and every later signal was shifted by one.
- The SIM and LIVE handler processes every bus signal. Only the harness had this lag.

**The fix:** the harness now drains any signal on every bar. It still waits up to 2 s on a `→IN_TRADE` transition, as before. The new `DealingRangeCarryTapeTest` replay does the same.

**Proof that it is a no-op on Main:** with the patched harness, Main fe1afec gives the same `candles.csv` md5 on both tapes:
- 7-day `73e666c43e3f6c6b1dd0e0aa6a787f1e`;
- 21-day `a590c1e57e4b609897f6ad526430336f`.

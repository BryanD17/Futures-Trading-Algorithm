## 1. THE DESIGN (every rule measurable; no gate weakened)

With `range.ltf.enabled=true` the HTF runner (the one the engine registers) constructs a **second, complete `StdvOteRunnerStrategy` in LTF mode** and feeds it every candle (primary and SMT) after its own step and after the counter-trend scalp. The child has its own detectors, its own `StdvOteStrategy` core + `SetupContext` (`machine = "LTF"`), its own `DealingRangeTracker` (window `INTRADAY_SWINGS`), its own M2b `PremiumDiscountEvaluator` (reading the LTF range), its own M7b gate, and the same `MandatoryConfluenceValidator` chain. It registers nothing: `StdvOteRegistry`, `HtfSeriesRegistry`, the PD / vote / 30m-OTE registries, `OteAgreementStats` and the checkpoint store keep reading the HTF machine. Its OTE events go to `OteEventLog` under `MNQ/LTF` and are not published on the bus. With the flag off the child is never built (`ltf == null`), and every added branch is guarded by `ltfMachine` / `ltf != null`.

| rule | implementation |
|---|---|
| LTF range (`range.ltf.window=INTRADAY_SWINGS`) | `DealingRangeTracker.SwingRange`: 1m bars → epoch-aligned 5m bars (final when the next bucket's first 1m bar arrives, the same moment the detector timeframe completes it). Swing = FractalSwings' strict formula, strength 2, confirmed 2 bars later. On each confirmed swing HIGH H the candidate leg is origin L = lowest 5m low since the previous confirmed swing high → H (mirror for a swing LOW). A candidate ≥ `minLegTicks × tick` REBUILDS the range to [L, H] with the leg's direction; smaller candidates change nothing (pullbacks inside the range). A leg whose later bars already closed back beyond its origin is not taken. |
| Extend / flip (HTF rules at the LTF scale) | Between swings on every 1m bar: a new extreme beyond the terminus extends it; a wick beyond the origin with the close back inside extends the origin (a sweep); a 1m CLOSE beyond the origin flips the direction (an opposite move of at least the range size, itself ≥ minLeg). Direction changes only through an opposite leg ≥ minLeg. |
| Carry | Swing history is not reset at the 18:00 ET roll: the LTF range carries across the reopen until a new qualifying leg replaces it. The 17:00-18:00 ET halt prints are ignored (same filter as the HTF tracker). |
| LTF bias (M2) | Recorded on every completed 5m detector bar: the LTF range's direction (NEUTRAL until a qualifying leg exists), through the same `recordHtfBias` hook (hysteresis grace, flip invalidation, emission invariant). The vote engine is never consulted. `INDEPENDENT`: that is the bias. `HTF_ALIGNED`: a direction ≠ the HTF machine's bias reads NEUTRAL (the machine does not hunt it). |
| Equilibrium / premium-discount (M2b) | The LTF machine's own PD evaluator reads the LTF range (`configureDealingRangeSource`), so M2b = entry vs the LTF equilibrium. `HTF_ALIGNED` adds `M2b: HTF_ALIGNED entry X not in the HTF discount/premium (HTF eq Y)`. |
| M2 re-check | Validator (machine `LTF` only): `M2: LTF range N ticks < range.ltf.minLegTicks M`; `HTF_ALIGNED`: `M2: LTF direction X != HTF bias Y (range.ltf.gating=HTF_ALIGNED)`. Confirmation: `M2: LTF machine bias=… range [lo,hi] N >= M ticks, gating=…`. |
| M4 / M5 / M6 / M7 | Unchanged pipeline on the LTF context: the sweep is scored by the LTF machine's own raid pipeline (floor 5), with the machine's own bias as the HTF term and the LTF equilibrium as the P/D factor. IMPULSE_LEG proves M5/M6 on the LTF leg bars (the same detectors) and arms on a sweep in the LTF OTE band (0.618 / 0.705 / 0.786 of the LTF range) reaching 0.705, PD array = ICT OB at the sweep, alarm on the rejection close; POST_SWEEP is the fallback (and, as on the HTF, draws the OTE on the post-sweep IMPULSE leg when the range leg is unusable, visible as `anchor=IMPULSE`). M7 RR band unchanged (floor 1.0 vs T1, ceiling 5.0 vs final). |
| Targets / stop | LTF ladder: **T1 = LTF equilibrium (0.5), T2 = LTF far edge** (the leg terminus); the signal carries the furthest rung within the ceiling. Stop = the unchanged anchored stop: beyond max(0.786, PD-array far edge, sweep extreme) + `stdvOte.stopBufferTicks` (4). |
| One position per symbol | Whichever machine emits first holds the symbol. The other may ARM (re-arm ignores the sibling's position) but not emit: HTF `POSITION: MNQ held by the LTF machine (…)`, LTF `STDV_OTE_LTF: POSITION: MNQ held by the HTF machine / account / counter-trend scalp (…)`. A `PositionClosedEvent` is routed to the owner (CT scalp, else the LTF latch, else the HTF latch). The CT scalp also refuses while the LTF latch is set. |
| Frequency / sessions / size | `range.ltf.maxPerDay` (4) per CME trading day (`LTF: range.ltf.maxPerDay N reached (k today)`); `range.ltf.sessions` (every open window; NO_ENTRY / WEEKEND dropped even if configured; the validator's M3 still blocks them); $ budget × `range.ltf.riskFraction` (1.0, clamped 0..1) through the SAME risk-derived sizer; the unchanged `PropFirmRiskEngine` decides. |
| Tagging | Signals: `STDV_OTE_LTF: <tier> size=N range=[legLow,legHigh] eq=… anchor=… T1=… T2=… RR(T1)=… RR=…`. Every LTF GateDecisionEvent reason starts `STDV_OTE_LTF:`; LTF-only gate `LTF` (sessions / quota). `SetupContext.machine` = `HTF` / `LTF`. Accessors: `getLtfContext()`, `getLtfRange()`, `getLtfRangeEvent()`, `machine()`. |

**Keys** (registered in `EngineConfig.KEYS`, set in `engine-defaults.properties`, boot line `LTF RANGE MACHINE (Agent 05.9): OFF (range.ltf.enabled=false)` / `ON …`, forwarded by `gradle/engine-config.gradle` under the new `range.` prefix):

## 9. OBSERVATIONS FOR FABLE

1. **Direction churn is the LTF machine's main failure mode.** On trend days the 120-tick LTF flips constantly and the setups die on their own bias flips (09-28). 200 ticks trades more and better on these tapes (+6.74R vs +2.84R on 21 days) because larger legs survive long enough; the sensitivity result is one tape, 28 days.
2. **Unfilled resting entries hold the symbol.** 5 of 20 LTF signals on the 21-day INDEPENDENT run never filled; each held the one-position latch until the setup expired / the SIM order TTL (80 bars) cancelled it. This is the HTF machine's lifecycle, unchanged (legacy target model has no entry-fill timeout); it cost the LTF, not the HTF, on these tapes.
3. **Size is always 5** on LTF entries: the tighter LTF stop gives a risk-derived size above the 5-contract cap, so `size.maxMicros` / the envelope bind as the brief requires. `range.ltf.riskFraction` < 1 is the lever if smaller LTF risk is wanted.
4. **POST_SWEEP fallback anchors.** When the LTF range leg is not usable for POST_SWEEP, the OTE is drawn on the post-sweep IMPULSE leg (as on the HTF). Such signals print `anchor=IMPULSE`; their "range" in the trade table is that impulse leg (e.g. 7-day 09-23 20:04, 20-pt leg).
5. **HTF_ALIGNED barely trades** (1-3 LTF signals per tape): most LTF setups that reach the OTE are against the HTF bias or on the wrong side of the HTF equilibrium. The owner's "own bias" requirement is what makes the machine trade at all.
6. **No gate was weakened (R3), the envelope is untouched (R4).** The LTF machine runs the unchanged M1..M9 chain on its own context; the only validator additions are stricter LTF re-checks.

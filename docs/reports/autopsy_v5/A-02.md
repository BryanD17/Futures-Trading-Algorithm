## BOUNDARY DECISIONS (alternatives exposed as config)

1. **All boundaries are ET, start-inclusive / end-exclusive**, converted from the candle
   instant with the tz database (`America/New_York`). CT = ET − 1 h and PT = ET − 3 h on
   every trading minute of 2026 (tested); the only exception is the DST transition hour on
   the Sunday mornings of 03-08 / 11-01, which is WEEKEND in every zone.
2. **NY_PM prime = 13:45–15:45 ET**, not 13:45–15:00. The task text says "prime
   13:45–15:00" AND "G1 2026-09-28 15:05 ET → NY_PM + prime=true"; both cannot hold. The
   "15:00" is the legacy NY PM killzone end in **CT** (KillzoneClock: 13:45–16:00 ET =
   12:45–15:00 CT), clipped by the SACRED 14:45 CT block → 15:45 ET. Constant:
   `SessionClassifier.NY_PM_PRIME_END = NO_ENTRY_START`. NY_AM prime 09:45–11:00 and
   London prime 02:00–05:00 exactly as specified (SCORING, all instruments; MGC's
   `scalp.londonPrime*` 03:00–05:00 still applies in BLOCKING scalp mode).
3. **Sunday 18:00–19:00 ET = PRE_ASIA** (like every weekday reopen hour), ASIA from 19:00.
   Identical to the harness's `session()` so histograms stay comparable. Entry-wise both
   are open, so the choice only affects labels.
4. **Friday**: 15:45–17:00 ET = NO_ENTRY, ≥ 17:00 ET = WEEKEND (no Friday Asia).
5. **NO_ENTRY / WEEKEND block M3 in BOTH modes.** In BLOCKING scalp mode the pre-V5 code
   OR-ed the NY PM prime killzone (to 16:00 ET = 15:00 CT) into the all-sessions window,
   i.e. it allowed entries 14:45–15:00 CT. That is now blocked at M3 (tightening only,
   rule R4). On the tape this changed 0 bars (BLOCKING csv byte-identical).
6. **Holidays** are not classified (no exchange calendar in scope); a holiday session is
   classified by clock like a normal day. `TradingSessionCalendar` untouched.
7. **Silver Bullet flag is false on WEEKEND/NO_ENTRY** (`classifyFull`); SilverBulletClock's
   own public API is unchanged (it never checked weekends and still does not).

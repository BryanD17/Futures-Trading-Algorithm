## DECISIONS

- **D1 Displacement (RC-09).** One detector: `DisplacementDetector.usePriorTrueRangeAtr(14)` — range ≥ `displacement.atrMult`
  (1.2) × mean TRUE range of the 14 PRIOR bars (current bar excluded: it cannot inflate the average it must beat), body ≥
  `displacement.bodyPct` (**0.50**, not the owner's 0.55: G1's body is 0.534 and must qualify). Owner's keys
  `stdvote.displacement.*` are honoured as aliases; new keys win. `displacement.recentBars` (12 detector bars) now exists on
  Main (was PR #151-only). A displacement must END after the sweep (sweep → displacement order).
- **D2 FVG linkage (RC-10).** FVG = 3-bar gap whose MIDDLE candle is the displacement or within `fvg.linkBars` (3) after it
  (or whose LAST candle is it). While the window is still printing the stall is `fvg-link-pending`; when it closes without a gap,
  the displacement's OB (last opposite-close candle, broken by a close within the window) is accepted as the M5 linkage
  (`ctx.m5LinkKind=OB`). Both FVG families agree on G1: `FvgDetector` [30620.75, 30630.00] stamped 15:00 ==
  `PdArrayLocator.linkedFvg` [30620.75, 30630.00] @15:00. (ictlib §S2 runs on 1m/15m only, not 5m.)
- **D3 PD arrays at M7.** Candidates = linked FVG, OB, unfilled same-direction FVGs, IFVGs (opposite FVG closed through),
  breakers (opposite OB closed through). A candidate qualifies when it OVERLAPS the band (D-19). Choice = entry level nearest the
  0.705 sweet spot (OB → mean threshold, gaps → first-touch edge, clamped into the band); ties OB > FVG > IFVG > BREAKER.
- **D4 MSS (M6).** One rule, one factory (`MarketStructureShiftDetector.forStdvOte()`): close beyond the MOST RECENT confirmed
  5m fractal swing (2 bars each side), each swing once, at/after the displacement bar, within `mss.freshBars` (30). On G1 the
  most recent fractal swing low is 30578.75 (14:35), so the MSS prints at 15:10, one bar after the owner's read (15:05 vs his
  30600.75 = the 14:30 bar low, which is not a fractal low). Within the ±1-bar tolerance; not hard-coded. A counter-bias MSS
  invalidates only when it breaks through the sweep extreme.
- **D5 OTE rules (RC-11/12).** Zone re-anchored every bar until ARM (the leg may still extend), then FROZEN. ARM = first 1m bar
  whose range trades into [0.618, 0.786] after MSS. ALARM = a candidate overlaps + reaction on that bar: close back beyond the
  0.618 edge toward the trade, a close in the trade direction inside the band, or a trade to the 0.705 (limit fill). EXPIRE =
  `ote.windowBars` (8 detector bars) in OTE_ARMED (runner) and `mss.freshBars` from MSS to ARM. INVALIDATE = CLOSE beyond the
  anchored 1.0. Fibs exactly 0.618 / 0.705 / 0.786 (`ote.fib62/fib705/fib79`).
- **D6 Planning.** Entry = PD-array level (G1 30635.50). Stop (`ote.stopMode=BAND`, default) = beyond the 0.786 or the
  PD array's far edge, whichever is further, + `stdvOte.stopBufferTicks` (4). `ORIGIN` restores the pre-V5 stop beyond the 1.0
  (used by the two scalp fixture tests whose fill choreography was built on it). Targets T1 = 0.5, T2 = 0.382, T3 = leg
  terminus; the signal carries the FURTHEST rung whose RR ≤ ceiling, with a real partial ladder {T1 50 %, final 50 %} and the
  real RR (not the tier default). Legacy `recordOteImpulse` path (unit tests only) keeps −2σ, stepping in to −1σ / −0.27σ when

## 2. THE RULES (every one measurable; no existing gate weakened)

`CounterTrendScalp` holds the pure rules and the scalp's own state. `StdvOteRunnerStrategy.stepCounterTrend` feeds it, **after** the with-trend steps, so the with-trend setup has priority on a shared bar. With the flag off the runner never constructs it (`counterTrend == null`).

| rule | implementation (numbers on the live range) |
|---|---|
| Direction | OPPOSITE the dealing-range direction. The engine's bias must equal the range direction: `CT: bias X disagrees with range Y`. |
| Where | The swept level must be in the range's premium half (short) or discount half (long): `CT: sweep L not in the premium half (eq E)`. It must also be inside that half's OTE band: `CT: sweep L not in the premium OTE band [30534.5,30578.75]`. The retrace extreme must stay inside the band and reach `ote.impulseLeg.minSweepFib` (0.705 → 30557.25), exactly as for IMPULSE_LEG. No close may print beyond the range 1.0. |
| Trigger | The IMPULSE_LEG trigger. A HIGH sweep (for a short) through THE sweep pipeline, with the scalp's own consumption markers. The PD array must be AT the sweep: the ICT order block (the newest up-close 1m bar among `ote.obLookbackBars` before the raid bar), the 5m sweep-bar OB, the rejection wick, or an FVG/IFVG/BREAKER that reaches the swept level. It must overlap the band, and the pick uses Agent 04's rule (nearest the 0.705). The alarm fires on the **rejection close** (`OteSetupDriver.impulseReaction`: close back below the swept level, down-close). |

## 3. M4: THE ONE DECISION FABLE MUST WEIGH (R3: re-parameterised for the CT entry only, with the tape numbers)

**The first implementation used the with-trend raid context verbatim, and it produced 0 scalps on both tapes.**

`RaidQualityScorer` gives every counter-trend sweep `✗ PENALTY: Opposing HTF Trend (-4)` and no `HTF Aligned (+2/+3)`. With the floor at 5, no counter-trend sweep can pass. Numbers:

| tape | sweeps in the scalp's OTE band (geometry passed) | scores | passed M4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7-day | 35 | 30 × 1, 5 × 2 | **0** |
| 21-day | 104 | 83 × 1, 17 × 2, 1 × 3, 3 × 4 | **0** |

A typical case, 7-day 2026-09-23 07:31 ET:
```
MNQ High Sweep @ Current Session High 31054.25 ... score=1
factors=[BASE (+1), Silver Bullet: London Open SB, Strong rejection (wick 84%) (+2),
         High swept in PREMIUM of dealing range (+1), PENALTY: Opposing HTF Trend (-4)]
```

**The fix:**
- The scalp's sweep is scored by THE pipeline (`RaidDetector.scoreSweep`, which is pure and registers nothing) with the HTF term **neutral** (`htfBullish = null`).
  - This excludes both the −4 penalty and the +2/+3 bonus.
  - The displacement-entry bonus is excluded too.
  - The SMT flag and the range equilibrium are this bar's.
- **The floor is unchanged (5)**, and the validator re-checks `raidScore >= 5`.
- Why this is correct:
  - The HTF term IS the with-trend rule that M2 adjudicates.
  - For this entry kind M2 is the CT rule, so counting the HTF term in M4 would double-count M2 and make the opt-in feature unreachable by construction.
- The with-trend sweeps, and their scores, are untouched.
- Both numbers are logged on every arm: `score 6 (HTF-neutral; with-trend context 2)`.
- Fable may prefer the literal floor. Then the answer to the owner is that "a counter-trend sweep never scores ≥ 5 on 28 days of tape".

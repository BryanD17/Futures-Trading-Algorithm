## DECISIONS

1. **Bias = the day's dealing range (RC-06).** New `DealingRangeTracker`. The range is built per CME day (18:00 ET roll) and ignores 17:00–18:00 halt prints. Direction is the most recent extreme once the range spans `bias.range.minRangePct` (0.08 % of price ≈ 24 pt on MNQ). A new extreme after a pullback of ≥ `bias.range.reanchorFraction` (0.5, into premium/discount) re-anchors the range (ICT "new dealing range after BOS"). A wick beyond the far extreme only extends it (a sweep). A **1m close** beyond it flips the leg. While the new day is undecided, the previous day's direction and range carry over. Tuning on the tape: 16–20 flips in 7 days. G1 is robust to minRangePct 0.08–0.15 and reanchor 0.5.
   Rejected alternatives: (a) a rolling-window H/L flips when old extremes roll off; (b) an un-reset BOS leg becomes a multi-day range (09-25 bullish still governing 09-28); (c) a fixed-size zigzag picks the 30356→30650 retrace as the "latest leg".
2. **Where the 15m structure sits.** `HtfTrendAnalyzer` is now just **V1**, one of four votes (V1 structure, V2 AMD, V3 true-day-open P/D, V4 daily draw). With `bias.source=RANGE` (default), a decisive dealing range anchors the final bias and the ADAPTIVE vote decides only while the range is not decisive. `bias.source=VOTE` gives the pure vote. `bias.vote.mode=LEGACY` gives the old 15m-structure bias. The vote still runs every 15m, and its disagreement with the legacy bias is counted (`agree=`).
3. **`bias.vote.mode` default LOG → VOTE.** Without this, the default engine (cfg A) would still trade the 15m retrace structure and G1 cannot pass. LOG and LEGACY stay one flag away.
4. **ADAPTIVE rule** (`bias.voteRule`, default ADAPTIVE): 4 voting → 3-of-4; 3 voting → 2-of-3; 2 voting → the warm pair must agree 2-0; fewer → NEUTRAL. STRICT_3OF4 keeps the §H1 rule exactly (`aggregate(votes)` is unchanged).
5. **V2 fix (PF-08).** `DailyAmdCycleTracker` now tests the phase transition against the **pre-candle** references and folds the candle into session extremes afterward. It rolls at 18:00 ET and ignores halt prints. The existing semantics of the lowest/highest reference are kept.
6. **Hysteresis ON by default, grace 3** (`bias.hysteresis`, alias `bias.hysteresis.enabled`; `bias.neutralGraceBars`). **biasEpoch** increments on every REAL flip, meaning a new non-NEUTRAL direction that differs from the last non-NEUTRAL one. NEUTRAL wobbles and repeats never move it. Epoch 1 opens on the first directional read. The emission-time safety invariant (current bias must be aligned) is untouched.
7. **M2b default LOG → BLOCK** (Appendix B lists M2b as BLOCKING). The governing range is now `RD`, the dealing range, ahead of R0/R1/R2. It is the same range the bias is read from, so a short is judged against the leg the bias comes from. ABSTAIN still always passes. `pd.gate.mode=LOG` restores logging only.
8. **Sweep resolution.** First choice is a LEVEL raid (a known level swept with rejection in the last 3 bars). Second is the LiquidityDetector swing sweep, scored by `RaidDetector.scoreSweep` against every level it took, else as a SESSION_HIGH/LOW swing level with no significance bonus. The higher score wins. Sweep direction comes from the SETUP's bias, the one M2 judges. The runner's raid context uses the same bias for the −4 opposing penalty. In SWEEP_DONE with no displacement yet, a **newer level raid** scoring ≥ the recorded sweep replaces it. This is how the 14:53 raid of 30640 becomes the setup's sweep.
9. **Level windows** (`levels.<asia|london|nyam|nypm|ny>.start|end`, HH:mm ET). Defaults: ASIA 20:00–00:00, **LONDON 04:00–06:00**, NY_AM 09:30–12:00, NY_PM 13:30–16:00, NY 09:30–16:00. 04:00–06:00 is the window whose high is the owner's 30640.00 on the tape. It is LuxAlgo's 01:00–03:00 London input evaluated in the owner's UTC-7 chart. The classic 02:00–05:00 window gives 30679.00. Window checks wrap midnight, which fixes the dead `hour < 0` Asia code. `inNY` now starts at 09:30 (it was 09:00). NY_AM_HIGH/LOW and NY_PM_HIGH/LOW are now emitted at window close.
10. **Phantom days** (`levels.minBarsPerDay`, default 60), 18:00 roll, halt bars ignored. Every session/day registration replaces the level object. The old "keep it when within tolerance" rule kept yesterday's raided object alive.
11. **Level re-arm** (`levels.rearmDistanceTicks`, default 100): a raided level is liquidity again once a whole candle has left it by that distance on the far side. Opening prices are never raided (they are not liquidity).
12. **Raid crossing rule.** A HIGH sweep needs the candle to trade from the level (low ≤ level + tolerance), and a LOW sweep mirrors this. Before, a bar printing entirely above an old high "raided" it.
13. **Tolerances in ticks.** `InstrumentRaidConfig.forSymbol("MNQ"|"MES"|"MGC")` now resolves to the NQ/ES/GC tick size. Before, micros fell through to the 0.01-tick default: a 0.03-pt tolerance and a 0.05-pt "minimum penetration".
14. **Manipulation leg** (`SessionLegLocator`). Own ET session windows: ASIA 18–02, LONDON 02–08, PRE_NY 08–09:30, NY_AM 09:30–12, NY_LUNCH 12–13:30, NY_PM 13:30–16, POST 16–18. So there is always a session, and nothing depends on Agent 02's `ctx.sessionWindow` field. Resolution order: JUDAS off the session open, then the LEVEL_SWEEP excursion that carried price from the session open through a known level, then the most-recent swing pair **immediately**. G1 at 14:53: `LEVEL_SWEEP 30578.75 → 30650.00` (NY_PM).
15. **Rollback switches (R3):** `bias.vote.mode=LEGACY`, `bias.hysteresis=false`, `pd.gate.mode=LOG`, `raid.sweepMode=LEGACY`, `manip.legMode=KILLZONE`, `raid.weights=V4`.

SECTION 5 — APPENDIX S: THE DETECTION SPECIFICATIONS (THE ONLY SOURCE)
================================================================================
These specifications describe standard ICT/SMC market concepts as pure
mathematics over OHLCV candles. They were written independently for this
project; implement from THIS text only (Licensing Guardrail). Conventions:
candle i has open o[i], high h[i], low l[i], close c[i]; i is the most
recently CLOSED candle; i-1 is one bar earlier. body[i] = |c[i]-o[i]|;
bodyTop[i] = max(o[i],c[i]); bodyBot[i] = min(o[i],c[i]);
wickTop[i] = h[i]-bodyTop[i]; wickBot[i] = bodyBot[i]-l[i].
All detections evaluate on candle close. All parameters are config with
the defaults shown. Default detection timeframe: the 1m feed unless a
family says otherwise; every family also accepts a configured timeframe
(run against the aggregated series) — defaults noted per family.

──────────────────────────────
§S1 — DISPLACEMENT CANDLE
──────────────────────────────
Parameters: meanLen=5, wickRatioMax=0.36.
Definitions:
  meanBody[i]  = SMA(body, meanLen) evaluated at i.
  smallWicks[i]= wickTop[i] < body[i]*wickRatioMax
             AND wickBot[i] < body[i]*wickRatioMax.
  displacementUp[i]   = body[i] > meanBody[i] AND smallWicks[i] AND c[i]>o[i].
  displacementDown[i] = body[i] > meanBody[i] AND smallWicks[i] AND c[i]<o[i].
Semantics: an expansion candle whose body dominates its range and exceeds
recent average bodies — the energetic move institutional flow leaves
behind. Emitted as a point detection (no zone), retained last 50.
State: none (instantaneous). Timeframe default: the strategy's LTF (1m
feed) AND the 15m aggregate (two registered instances).

──────────────────────────────
§S2 — FAIR VALUE GAP (FVG) / INVERSE (IFVG)
──────────────────────────────
Parameters: mode=FVG|IFVG (default FVG), retainPerSide=10 (chart shows
fewer; registry keeps 10), timeframe default 1m and 15m instances.
Detection at close of candle i (the three-candle pattern is i-2, i-1, i):
  Bullish FVG: displacementUp[i-1] AND l[i] > h[i-2].
    Zone = [h[i-2], l[i]]  (bottom, top). Created at bar i.
  Bearish FVG: displacementDown[i-1] AND h[i] < l[i-2].
    Zone = [h[i], l[i-2]].
  IFVG mode inverts the gap comparisons (l[i] < h[i-2] / h[i] > l[i-2])
  producing overlap ("implied") zones; displacement requirement stays.
Consecutive-gap merge: if a same-direction FVG was created at bar i-1 and
another qualifies at bar i, EXTEND the existing zone to the union of the
two zones instead of creating a second detection.
Lifecycle (bullish; bearish mirrors with highs):
  ACTIVE  : price has not re-entered the zone.
  TOUCHED : l[j] < zoneTop for some later bar j (partial fill).
  FILLED  : l[j] < zoneBottom (full traverse) -> terminal; freeze right
            edge at bar j.
State transitions are monotonic (never revert). Retention: cap per side;
oldest terminal detections evicted first.

──────────────────────────────
§S3 — BALANCED PRICE RANGE (BPR)
──────────────────────────────
Parameters: retainPerSide=5.
Definition: when the most recent ACTIVE bullish FVG and most recent
ACTIVE bearish FVG overlap in price, the intersection is a BPR:
  region = [max(bullBottom, bearBottom_intersect_low), ...] — precisely:
  low  = max(bullZoneBottom, bearZoneBottom)
  high = min(bullZoneTop,   bearZoneTop);   valid iff low < high.
Orientation: BULLISH BPR when the bullish gap sits above the bearish
gap's bottom with the bullish gap created LATER (upward repricing through
a prior bearish gap); BEARISH BPR mirrors. If both orderings are
ambiguous on a given pair, orientation = direction of the LATER-created
gap.
Position flag at creation: pos = +1 if close > region.high, -1 if close <
region.low, else 0 — determines which side invalidates:
Lifecycle: ACTIVE; TOUCHED when price re-enters the region from the pos
side; BROKEN (terminal) when price closes fully through to the far side.
Extend right edge while ACTIVE; freeze on BROKEN.

──────────────────────────────
§S4 — VOLUME IMBALANCE (VI)
──────────────────────────────
Parameters: retain=6, projectBars=3 (display only).
A body-gap between consecutive candles whose wicks still overlap (a
visible inefficiency that is not a full opening gap).
Bullish VI between i-1 and i — ALL must hold:
  (1) o[i] > c[i-1]          (opens above prior close)
  (2) c[i] > c[i-1]          (closes above prior close)
  (3) o[i] > o[i-1]          (opens above prior open)
  (4) h[i-1] > l[i]          (wicks overlap — not an opening gap)
  (5) h[i-1] < bodyBot[i]    (prior wick high below current body)
  Zone = [bodyTop[i-1], bodyBot[i]]  (bottom, top).
Bearish VI mirrors:
  o[i] < c[i-1]; c[i] < c[i-1]; o[i] < o[i-1]; l[i-1] < h[i];
  l[i-1] > bodyTop[i]. Zone = [bodyTop[i], bodyBot[i-1]].
Lifecycle: ACTIVE; FILLED (terminal) when a later candle's range fully
covers the zone. Note test guidance: each inequality gets a near-miss
negative test — these five conditions are the easiest place to introduce
a silent bug.

──────────────────────────────
§S5 — OPENING GAPS (WEEKLY / DAILY)
──────────────────────────────
Parameters: retainWeekly=3, retainDaily=2.
Session calendar: the engine's existing trading-day rollover (18:00 ET
session boundary; weekly = the first session of the trading week, which
opens Sunday 18:00 ET; if the D1 ladder from V3 exists, use its calendar
— one calendar, one truth).
WEEKLY OPENING GAP: zone between the final trade price of the previous
week's last session (its close) and the first session's open:
  low = min(prevWeekClose, weekOpen); high = max(...); midline =
  avg(prevWeekClose, weekOpen). Created at the week-open bar. A holiday-
  shifted week keys on SESSIONS, not calendar days.
DAILY OPENING GAP: same construction between previous session close and
current session open.
Lifecycle: ACTIVE (extend right); TOUCHED when price enters the zone;
no terminal state by fill (these act as persistent magnets) — eviction
is by retention count only (oldest evicted). Midline is part of the
detection meta (it is the commonly-watched magnet level).

──────────────────────────────
§S6 — LIQUIDITY POOLS (CLUSTERED EQUAL SWINGS)
──────────────────────────────
Parameters: swingLen=5 (left bars; right confirmation=1),
toleranceDiv=2.5 (tolerance = ATR(10)/toleranceDiv), minCluster=3,
scanDepth=50, retainPerSide=4.
Swing points: pivot high at bar p iff h[p] is strictly greater than the
prior swingLen highs and greater than the 1 following high (confirmed one
bar later). Pivot low mirrors. Maintain a rolling list of confirmed
swings (bounded scanDepth).
Cluster test on each NEW confirmed pivot P (price y):
  among prior SAME-SIDE swings, walk newest to oldest; stop at the first
  one farther than tolerance from y ON THE FAR SIDE (cluster must be
  contiguous in recency); count those within [y-tolerance, y+tolerance].
  If count (including P) >= minCluster:
    poolPrice = avg(minClusterPrice, maxClusterPrice);
    zone = [poolPrice - tolerance, poolPrice + tolerance];
    anchored at the OLDEST swing in the cluster.
  If a pool already exists anchored at the same oldest swing, UPDATE its
  zone instead of creating a duplicate.
Sides: pools built from swing highs = BUYSIDE liquidity (resting stops
above); from swing lows = SELLSIDE.
Lifecycle (buyside; sellside mirrors): ACTIVE; PARTIAL when close >
zoneBottom... precisely: PARTIAL when close crosses the NEARER boundary
(bottom for buyside approached from below -> use: brokenLow when close >
zone.low; brokenHigh when close > zone.high); SWEPT (terminal) when BOTH
boundaries have been closed through. On PARTIAL, the pool is "being
raided"; on SWEPT it is consumed.
INTEGRATION (Agent 03): each pool registers into LevelEngine as an
equal-high/low KnownLevel tagged ICTLIB_CLUSTER; the raid pipeline treats
it exactly like its native equal levels.

──────────────────────────────
§S7 — ORDER BLOCKS WITH BREAKER LIFECYCLE
──────────────────────────────
Parameters: swingLen=10, useBody=true (zone from bodies vs full wicks),
retainPerSide=5.
Swing regime: maintain the most recent confirmed swing high (top) and
swing low (btm) using swingLen-bar confirmation; each carries a
"crossed" flag, initially false.
Bullish OB creation: on the first close c[i] > top.price with top not
yet crossed:
  mark top.crossed; scan bars from top.bar+1 to i-1 for the candle with
  the LOWEST low-reference (low-ref = bodyBot if useBody else l); the OB
  zone = [that candle's low-ref, that candle's high-ref] where high-ref =
  bodyTop if useBody else h; anchored at that candle. (The origin of the
  move that broke structure.)
Bearish OB mirrors on first close below an uncrossed btm (scan for the
HIGHEST high-ref).
Lifecycle:
  ACTIVE  : freshly created demand (bullish) / supply (bearish) zone.
  TESTED  : price trades into the zone without a body-close through it.
  BREAKER : a body-close through the zone's far edge (bullish OB: min(o,c)
            < zone.bottom) — polarity flips; the zone becomes resistance
            (bullish breaker) / support (bearish breaker).
  REMOVED : (terminal) after BREAKER, a close beyond the zone's near edge
            in the original direction (bullish: close > zone.top) — the
            zone is spent.
Retention per side; terminal evicted first.

──────────────────────────────
§S8 — STRUCTURE ENGINE (MSS / BOS)
──────────────────────────────
Parameters: pivotLeft=5, pivotRight=1, historyCap=200.
Zigzag: alternate confirmed pivot highs/lows (same confirmation as §S6);
consecutive same-side pivots keep only the more extreme.
Regime dir ∈ {-1, 0, +1}, initial 0.
MSS (shift): close crosses ABOVE the most recent confirmed swing HIGH
while dir <= 0 -> dir := +1, emit MSS_BULL at that swing's price/bar.
Mirror for MSS_BEAR (close below most recent swing low while dir >= 0).
BOS (continuation): while dir == +1, each close above a NEWER confirmed
swing high emits BOS_BULL — deduplicated: no emission if the level equals
the previous MSS/BOS level for that side. Mirror bearish.
Emitted as point detections with price + bar; bounded by historyCap.
Note: this engine serves the chart and confluence stack. The 2022-model
gate MSS is a different, stricter construct and remains untouched;
[ICTLIB-DIFF] measures their divergence.

──────────────────────────────
§S9 — TREND-SHIFT OTE ANCHORING (ChartEngine alternative mode)
──────────────────────────────
Parameters: pivotLen=10 (both sides), band = engine default [0.62, 0.79]
(config-overridable; the widely-published variant is [0.618, 0.786]).
Timeframe: the ChartEngine's 30m series (unchanged).
Swing tracking: confirmed pivot highs/lows with pivotLen bars on each
side; keep last and previous per side.
Trend shift:
  BULL shift: a new confirmed pivot high STRICTLY greater than the
  previous swing high, while trendState <= 0 -> trendState := +1;
  anchors: origin = most recent confirmed swing LOW (price+bar),
  extreme = the new pivot high.
  BEAR shift mirrors (new pivot low below previous swing low while
  trendState >= 0; origin = last swing high, extreme = new low).
Anchor extension: while trendState == +1 and no new shift, each new
confirmed pivot high ABOVE the current extreme moves the extreme (fibs
re-stretch). Extension only re-stretches a zone still in FORMING; an
ARMED/REACTED zone is never silently re-anchored (a tagged band is a
fact; re-stretching it would rewrite history — instead, a post-ARM
extension INVALIDATES the old zone and forms a new one).
Fib convention: identical to the engine's existing one — 0.0 at the
EXTREME, 1.0 at the ORIGIN; OTE band per config.
Invalidation: close through the origin (existing rule) OR an opposite
trend shift.
Lifecycle: reuse OteState exactly (FORMING/ARMED/REACTED/INVALIDATED/
EXPIRED) — no new states, no consumer changes.

APPENDIX E — DESIGN RATIONALE (WHY EACH PIECE IS SHAPED THIS WAY)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  E1. WHY SPEC-FIRST INSTEAD OF PORTING.
      Beyond the license: Pine is a rendering language over a chart
      runtime — boxes, labels, per-bar repaint. A trading engine needs
      FACTS with lifecycles, queryable at decision time. Appendix S is
      what the indicators MEAN, stripped of what they DRAW. The Java
      implementation is therefore simpler, testable (Appendix W), and
      faster than any transliteration could be.

  E2. WHY ictlib IS PARALLEL, NOT A REPLACEMENT.
      The gate detectors are tuned to the 2022-model state machine; a
      hot-swap would change live trading semantics in one PR — exactly
      what the Rollout Doctrine forbids. Parallel + [ICTLIB-DIFF] gives
      the owner a measured unification decision later.

  E3. WHY THE DISPLACEMENT REQUIREMENT ON FVGs MATTERS.
      Naive three-candle gaps fire constantly on quiet tape; requiring a
      displacement middle candle keeps only gaps left by energetic
      moves — fewer, better zones. Expect the FVG count to DROP; that is
      the feature (Troubleshooting entry exists so nobody "fixes" it).

  E4. WHY PROFILES INSTEAD OF LOOSENING GATES.
      Deleting a gate is unrecoverable context; a profile is a named,
      reversible, testable configuration with risk pinned outside it.
      And the always-on simulator means even in STRICT, every session
      quietly answers "what would STANDARD have done?" — the owner never
      has to guess what a flip would change.

  E5. WHY MINIMAL EXISTS AT ALL.
      Not to trade — to diagnose. MINIMAL=0 would-trades proves the
      problem is upstream (warmth, data, sweep detection), not gate
      strictness. It bounds the debate with a number.

  E6. WHY TRI-STATE CONFLUENCE FIELDS.
      A cold source reported as FALSE poisons the score and quietly
      recreates the starved-input failure class. UNKNOWN excluded from
      maxScore keeps a cold stack visibly cold and the doctrine intact.

  E7. WHY THE POST-ARM RE-ANCHOR RULE (§S9).
      A tagged OTE band is a historical fact used for entries; silently
      re-stretching it after ARM would let the zone "chase" price and
      make REACTED unfalsifiable. Invalidate-and-reform keeps every zone
      auditable against the chart.

  E8. WHY POOLS REGISTER INTO LevelEngine.
      Two level universes means a raid can fire on a level the chart
      never showed, or vice versa — undebuggable. One universe, source-
      tagged, keeps raids, chart, and confluence telling one story.
